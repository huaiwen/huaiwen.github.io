Biography

I am a Professor and Ph.D. supervisor at the College of Computer Science, Inner Mongolia University. My research focuses on trustworthy artificial intelligence, multimodal intelligence, and media content security. I have been selected for a national young talent program and received the Inner Mongolia Distinguished Young Scholars Fund. I also hold an A3 research position under IMU's Junma Program.

I received my Ph.D. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I worked in the Multimedia Computing Group, National Laboratory of Pattern Recognition, advised by Changsheng Xu, Shengsheng Qian, and Quan Fang. I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from Inner Mongolia University in 2016.

Research Interests

  • Trustworthy AI and safety: AI value systems, value alignment, machine unlearning, and model hallucinations.
  • Multimodal generation and retrieval: multimodal representation learning, controllable generation, and generative retrieval.
  • Media content security: rumor detection, fact-checking, and deepfake detection.
  • Social media intelligence: opinion mining, cognitive communication, social simulation, and intelligent agents.
